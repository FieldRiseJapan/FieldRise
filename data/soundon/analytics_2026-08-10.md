## Updated analysis — important findings
1. TikTok distribution continues to accelerate: videos increased from **1,062 on 2026-08-11 → 1,275 on 2026-08-12 → 1,586 on 2026-08-14**.
2. TikTok views increased from **1,410.5万 → 1,526.0万 → 1,739.0万**, while likes rose from **71.4万 → 77.8万 → 90.1万**.
3. Unlike the earlier 8/11 plateau, cumulative music plays increased to **7,166 on 8/12** and **7,896 on 8/14**, while listeners increased to **202 → 217**. Downstream music consumption has resumed growth.
4. The main `cafe` track reached **361 videos / 936.5万 views / 613 plays / 174 listeners** and remains the strongest visible listener-producing track.
5. `cafe (Rainy day...)` expanded to **1,155 videos / 792.5万 views / 2,128 plays / 10 listeners**. It remains the dominant high-volume distribution pattern, but unique-listener conversion is still weak.
6. `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` reached **37 videos / 1.6万 views / 854 plays**. Its music-play efficiency remains exceptionally high relative to its tiny video/view footprint, making it a key control track for conversion analysis.
7. Saves increased from **10 → 13** by 8/12 and remained 13 on 8/14. This is improvement, but saving/conversion remains much weaker than TikTok reach and likes.
8. The data now supports three distinct signals: **Rainy Day = distribution scale**, **A couple gazing = music-play efficiency**, **main cafe = unique-listener generation**.
9. The latest 8/14 data is especially useful for future SUNO AI experiments because the Cafe series now has enough separation between distribution, engagement, plays, and listeners to define different optimization targets rather than chasing views alone.

## Recommended research focus for FieldRise Music AI
- Treat `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` as a high-conversion/high-play-efficiency candidate and investigate its musical structure, opening seconds, mood, visual pairing, and video context.
- Treat `cafe (Rainy day...)` as a high-distribution candidate and investigate what drives its unusually large number of TikTok videos.
