## Updated analysis — important findings
1. TikTok distribution continues to accelerate: videos increased from **839 → 1,062** and views from **1,293.6万 → 1,410.5万**.
2. Likes increased from **65.2万 → 71.4万**, maintaining very strong engagement growth.
3. Cumulative music plays and listeners shown on the dashboard remained **6,438 / 169**, despite continued TikTok growth. This strengthens the need to distinguish social reach from downstream music consumption.
4. The main `cafe` track reached **271 videos / 794.3万 views / 494 plays / 138 listeners** and remains the strongest visible listener-producing track.
5. `cafe (Rainy day...)` expanded strongly to **729 videos / 607.5万 views / 1,660 plays**, confirming it as the dominant distribution pattern. Its listener count remains only 7, so reach is not translating proportionally into unique listeners.
6. `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` remains a high-efficiency candidate with **36 videos / 1.6万 views / 628 plays**. It should remain a priority control track for music-play conversion analysis.
7. Saves remain at only **10**, making saves the clearest weak downstream-conversion signal relative to the rapidly growing TikTok reach and likes.
8. The current dataset supports two distinct Cafe success patterns: **high-volume distribution (Rainy Day)** and **high music-consumption efficiency from a small footprint (A couple gazing...)**.

## Recommended research focus for FieldRise Music AI
- Treat `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` as a high-conversion candidate and investigate its musical structure, opening seconds, mood, visual pairing, and video context.
- Treat `cafe (Rainy day...)` as a high-distribution candidate and investigate what drives its large number of TikTok videos.
- Do not optimize only for views. Track the full funnel: videos → views → likes → music plays → listeners → saves.
- Use these two Cafe patterns as separate control groups for future SUNO AI prompt experiments.
