## Analysis — important findings
1. TikTok views increased from 602.7万 to 1,293.6万 between the 2026-08-05 and 2026-08-10 snapshots (about 2.15x).
2. Music plays increased from 945 to 5,564 (about 5.89x), substantially faster than TikTok views.
3. Listeners increased from 45 to 146 (about 3.24x).
4. Likes increased from 18.3万 to 65.2万 (about 3.56x).
5. Saves remained at 10 across the displayed snapshots, indicating that saving behavior did not rise in proportion to reach/engagement.
6. The Cafe series is the dominant visible growth driver. In particular, the Rainy Day variant reached 548 videos and 558.9万 views in the 2026-08-10 snapshot.
7. `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` is notable because it generated 438 plays from only 36 videos and about 1.5万 views, suggesting strong music-play conversion despite a small distribution footprint.
8. The current pattern suggests a transition from TikTok discovery into measurable music consumption, making the Cafe series a high-value case study for future FieldRise Music AI prompt/production analysis.
