3. The main `cafe` track reached **394 videos / 987.3万 views / 649 plays / 186 listeners** and remains the strongest visible listener-producing track.
4. `cafe (Rainy day...)` expanded to **1,230 videos / 897.4万 views / 2,549 plays / 13 listeners**. It remains the dominant high-volume distribution pattern, and its music plays continue to grow, but unique-listener conversion remains comparatively weak.
5. `cafe (A couple gazing into each other's...)` reached **37 videos / 1.6万 views / 962 plays**. Its music-play efficiency remains exceptionally high relative to its tiny video/view footprint, making it a key control track for conversion analysis.
6. Saves remained at **13** despite the large increase in views and likes. Save conversion remains a clear downstream weakness.
7. The data continues to support three distinct Cafe signals: **Rainy Day = distribution scale**, **A couple gazing = music-play efficiency**, **main cafe = unique-listener generation**.
8. `Shopping and jazz` reached **450.4 plays** despite zero TikTok videos in the visible table, indicating that music consumption can occur independently of TikTok video volume and should be tracked separately from social distribution.
9. The 8/15 snapshot strengthens the case for optimizing different tracks against different targets rather than using views as the single success metric.
