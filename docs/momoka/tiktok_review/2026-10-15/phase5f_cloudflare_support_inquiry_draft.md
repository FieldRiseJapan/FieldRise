Subject: Workers/D1/R2 — commercial Free usage and enforceable zero-charge conditions

Hello Cloudflare Support,

We are evaluating hosting for a small business web application in Japan before registering or purchasing any subscription. We cannot authorize additional usage charges. Please distinguish:
(1) No charge within the free allowance.
(2) Usage beyond the allowance is rejected without automatic charges.
(3) Usage alone cannot switch the configuration into a paid plan or metered billing.

1. What terms permit commercial use of Workers/D1 Free, and what happens at the limits? Which services can be used without a payment method?
2. Does starting R2 require a subscription checkout and a valid payment method? Is there a supported configuration that starts and continues operating R2 without one?
3. Is there a contractual or platform-enforced zero-dollar hard cap for R2? Please cover storage, Class A/B operations, retrieval and other dimensions, all buckets/account-wide usage, concurrency, propagation and behavior at the cap.
4. We understand that Budget Alerts do not cap usage and Threshold billing collects accrued charges. Is there another setting, API or contractual evidence ensuring that higher usage cannot incur charges?
5. Are R2 and other metered subscriptions separate from Workers Free? Please identify any configuration including Cron, Queues, Secrets, monitoring and backups that cannot become billable without an explicit purchase or upgrade.
6. If no hard cap or guarantee exists, please state this and suggest supported no-charge alternatives that do not rely on failed payments or removing a payment method.

Please provide official documentation, applicable plans/region, confirmation date, coverage and exclusions. If account details are necessary, explain why and the secure channel. We will not supply credentials, payment details or user records. This is an information request, not authorization to change configuration.

Thank you.
