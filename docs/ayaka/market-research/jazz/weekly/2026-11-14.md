## Suno Style案

用途は朝のコーヒーや英語の語り動画。Cafe 001の冒頭の低音を参考に、ピアノの音数を控える案。BPMは制作の仮設定で、市場最適値は未確認。

```text
Slow intimate cafe jazz instrumental, 50 BPM. Begin with a warm upright bass motif alone for the first two seconds. Let a soft felt piano enter with a sparse, memorable melody and generous space between notes. Keep the accents gentle, with no repeated-note runs, no claps and minimal percussion. Leave room for spoken voice and make the ending loop naturally into the opening bass motif.
```
