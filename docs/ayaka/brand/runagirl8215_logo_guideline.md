# Runa-Girl8215 Logo / Brand Guideline

## Official visual direction

The current Runa-Girl8215 logo design approved by the president is the standard reference for future image creation and SNS content.

### Logo
- Brand name: `Runa-Girl8215`
- Typography: elegant, refined script / handwritten-style wordmark
- Placement: lower-right of the image by default
- Motif: small, elegant crown above or near the wordmark
- Main treatment: white lettering with restrained gold accents
- Overall mood: chic, sophisticated, premium, calm; not flashy or overly cute
- Decoration: subtle musical note / fine ornamental line / minimal sparkle may be used
- The logo must remain clearly legible at SNS viewing sizes
- Preserve the underlying image's atmosphere; the logo should enhance the visual rather than dominate it

## Official tagline

`You are the moment.`
`Music makes it shine.`

Concept in Japanese:
「あなたが主役。音楽は、その瞬間を輝かせる。」

## Brand concept

Runa-Girl8215 positions music as an enhancer of the viewer's / creator's moment rather than the thing that steals the spotlight.

The person, story, stream, scene, or video is the star. The music supports the atmosphere and makes that moment shine.

This concept should remain consistent across:
- TikTok
- Instagram
- YouTube
- Streamer-oriented Cafe series
- Commercial Music Licensing (CML)
- Future promotional / advertising use

## Standard composition

Default lower-right lockup:

Runa-Girl8215
You are the moment.
Music makes it shine.

Use the current approved logo appearance as the visual reference whenever the president asks to add the Runa-Girl8215 logo to an image.

## Usage rule for future automation

When an image is supplied for SNS posting, the image-processing workflow should be able to apply this standard logo treatment automatically unless the president explicitly requests another treatment.

Do not replace the approved tagline with unrelated promotional copy unless specifically instructed.
